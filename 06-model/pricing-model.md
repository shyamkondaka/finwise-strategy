# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 2 · Revenue Expansion, 1. Conversion is stuck at ~2% across 13 months while traffic, trial volume and session duration all varied widely. A rate that doesn't move with volume points to a structural problem in where value and the paywall meet, not a traffic problem. 2. 60% of paying customers churn within a year. Even users who pay don't keep realizing value, so pricing changes alone can't fix growth. Any pricing change has to be tied to activation.

**Model:** Freemium, Adoption and data continuity vs short-term ARPU. Ending the trial in a hard lockout [ASSUMED current behavior] throws away the connected data and history that make FinWise more valuable over time, along with 98% of trial accounts. Usage-based pricing (per transaction or connected account) would track value, but it penalizes small businesses with uneven cash flow and makes bills unpredictable, which matters most to a finance-tool buyer. Giving up some free-tier revenue keeps future buyers inside the product.

_____

## Recommendation

For new trial accounts: move the paywall from "trial ends" to "collaborative finance workflows." Keep core single-user bookkeeping free after the 14-day reverse trial [ASSUMED length], and keep inviting one accountant/bookkeeper free on every tier. Paid unlocks the workflows that collaboration creates: approval flows, shared month-end reporting, automated reconciliation and multiple connected accounts.

**The pricing bet:** Packaging matched to value. Hypothesis: if we gate collaborative workflows instead of time, trial-to-paid conversion rises from 2% to ≥3% within 90 days of signup, and 1-year retention for converted accounts beats 40%. Explicitly not a price increase: raising prices on a product most users don't activate would hurt conversion further.

_____

## Model signal

Targets the 7-day activation rate and the % of accounts with 2+ active users (from the Module 4 experiment brief). A free accountant invite removes the cost barrier to the behavior most linked to reaching value. The paywall then sits where a business has become operationally dependent, so conversions should cluster at the free/paid boundary instead of being lost when the trial clock runs out.
